One board, every medium

Everything a long project touches.

No switching between six apps to follow one thought. Every kind of file sits on the same canvas, at the size you want, beside what it belongs with.

Notes

Markdown, rendered as you type: headings, tables, links, images. .md

Documents

PDFs and EPUBs, read in place and searchable. .pdf .epub

Images

Photos and references at full resolution.

Audio

Recordings with a waveform you can scrub.

Video

Clips and footage, playing on the board.

Boards & to-dos

Kanban and checklists, as plain files.

Drawings

Sketch straight onto a card.

Code

Source files, beside the notes about them.

Why Tephra

Built to be yours, for years.

01

Local-first

No account, no subscription, no cloud, no telemetry. Your work stays on your machine.

02

Open formats

Markdown, JSON and the files you already have. Readable with or without Tephra.

03

Few, sharp tools

Cards, links, groups and canvases — simple pieces that combine.

04

Fast at any size

The work done is proportional to what's on screen, not to how big the project has grown.
